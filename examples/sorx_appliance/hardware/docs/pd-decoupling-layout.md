# PD internal-regulator decoupling layout

**NOT READY FOR FABRICATION.** This is a verified geometry improvement, not a PD startup or transient measurement.

STUSB4500 datasheet DS12499 revision 8, sections 2.2.13 and 2.2.15, specifies that VREG_1V2 and VREG_2V7 are for external decoupling only. Each needs a nominal 1 µF capacitor, with an allowed effective range of 0.5 to 10 µF. Table 1 connects the exposed pad to GND. Source: [STUSB4500 official datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stusb4500.pdf). The project names these nets PD_1V2 and PD_2V7, with capacitors C14 and C15. They are protected local power loops, not ordinary signals for automatic completion routing. Effective capacitance versus DC bias, temperature and tolerance remains a component qualification item.

The actual native source-to-destination audit reproduced a 20.465 mm planar path and four vias from U2.21 to C14.1, and a 16.455 mm planar path from U2.23 to C15.1. Those were unnecessarily long regulator decoupling paths. The corrected placement is C14 at (17.75, 9.25), 90 degrees, B.Cu; C15 at (20.75, 11.5), 0 degrees, B.Cu. It retains the legal front-side pin escapes and shortens the planar connections to 4.880 and 7.170 mm respectively, with one signal via each. These geometric lengths include pad-center paths but exclude barrel length; they are not extracted loop inductance.

The exact old PD net copper is removed by UUID, including obsolete branches and the internal crossing that prevented the U2 ground connection. The C14 transition is reused. C15 receives one 0.60/0.30 mm copper/drill transition. Each capacitor ground receives a nearby ground via. U2's exposed pad receives three 0.60/0.30 mm ground vias at (16, 12.5), (15, 13.5) and (16, 13.5). They connect the surface ground land to the ground planes and enable the required native connectivity closure.

The initial four-site engineering proposal was rejected: (15, 12.5) intersects the PD_AUX_ADJ trace on B.Cu. That site is omitted, with the exact native clearance/drill evidence retained. A first capacitor placement also overlapped R12's courtyard and was corrected. ST does not prescribe this particular four-site pattern; three accepted sites are an engineering implementation, not a waiver of a manufacturer array requirement. The U13 thermal-via pattern is a separate Microchip requirement and must not be conflated with this U2 choice.

These exposed-pad vias require an agreed filled/capped process or another supplier-approved solder-loss treatment, plus stencil and assembly review. A simple assertion that a hole is tented does not establish top-side soldermask coverage within a fully opened exposed pad. Signal-pad via-in-pad remains prohibited unless separately justified.

`tools/relocate_pd_decoupling.py` preserves the exact pin escapes, removes only the obsolete PD-net copper, and performs a fresh-process native DRC comparison. The first accepted isolated proof reduced 54 required opens to 53 and 113 other findings to 112, with zero new findings. Before/after endpoint paths, rejected candidates and commands are retained under `validation/pd-decoupling-proof-01/` and `validation/pd-decoupling-proof-02/`. Final integration repeats the check against the shared board; counts from this isolated proof are not final release counts.
