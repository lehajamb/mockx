## 1. Goal as understood

Hardware-only design of a single-board smart order router appliance (SORX): a custom KiCad 7 carrier around a US-available SoM, with three role-separated GbE ports (market data in, order entry, management), PPS input, RTC, console, USB-C PD power, watchdog, status LEDs, and a half-width 1U printed enclosure. Deliverables in one repo (`hw/`, `mech/`, `bom/`, `docs/`), verified without hardware: ERC/DRC with zero unwaived errors, enclosure fit check, dated BOM stock check. End target: US-sourceable and ready to manufacture.

## 4. Decisions and why

- **Verdin iMX8M Plus Quad 4GB IT (0063)**: two native GbE MACs with 1588, one TSN-capable; user approved.
- **Port mapping**: MD-IN on SoM ETH_1, management on SoM ETH_2 + KSZ9131, order entry on an Intel I210-AT over PCIe. Gives three hardware-timestamping ports.
- **I210 on the bottom side**: its MDI pin order is only planar to the jack from below.
- **No USB hubs**: USB 1 is USB 3.0, USB 2 is USB 2.0 and doubles as the recovery port, debug USB-C is console only. Differs from the checkpoint diagram.
- **STUSB4500 PD sink**: NVM-configured; needs programming for 2 A. Differs from the checkpoint diagram.
- **Scripted KiCad flow**: one model generates schematic, placement and BOM, so they cannot drift apart.
- **Freerouting 1.9.0**: 2.1.0 ignores the pass limit and writes no result unless fully routed.
- **GND not autorouted**: connected afterwards by pours and stitching vias.
- **Stackup changed today**: In1 solid GND; In2 now carries supply rails with GND fill around them. Reason: supply tracks on the outer layers were choking the area around the PHY. Cost: pairs on B.Cu cross supply routes in their reference layer.
- **Wider placement spacing**: with 0.45 mm spacing no via fitted next to the QFNs, so their pins could not escape.

## 5. Open problems, bugs, dead ends

Open connections in `sorx_routed.kicad_pcb` (13): MG_LED2 ×2, VBUS_DBG_RAW ×2, USB1_OC#, USB1_VBUS_DET, USB1_ID, PPS_GPIO, MG_TXD0, PWR_FLT#, +1V2_PHY, OE_0V9, OE_JTAG_TCK, DBG_CC1.
- About six are long connections; my router gave up on them only because of its search limit.
- MG_TXD0, MG_LED2 (U13 pins 19, 15) and VBUS_DBG_RAW (J10 B4) are fenced in by other tracks.
- +1V2_PHY at U13 pin 4 is fenced in by the locked MDI pairs. It cannot be fixed without re-running pair routing; the fan-out via code in `route.py` is meant for this.

Known bugs:
- `finish.py ground` puts stitching vias inside the microSD footprint's keep-out (4 DRC errors on the test run).
- `finish.py fixgnd` only sees pad-named items and misses pour islands; `patch_route.py gnd` replaces it.
- `docs/stackup-and-fab-notes.md` still describes In2 as a GND plane.
- `mech/` models and `hw/build/mech_parts.json` predate the new placement.

Quality gaps:
- Pairs are autorouted, not length-matched. Worst intra-pair skew: DBG_D 15.3 mm, OE_MDI3 10.8 mm, USB2_D 6.6 mm, MG_MDI3 4.4 mm, OE_MDI1 4.4 mm, PCIE_RX 2.4 mm.
- Supply nets are widened tracks, not pours.
- SODIMM footprint is derived, not taken from the TE drawing; module standoffs are not fitted.
- Release gates G1–G5 in `docs/design-review-notes.md` are all open.

Dead ends, do not repeat:
- Freerouting 2.1.0 for phase 2.
- Wide supply classes in the autorouter (it cannot start from fine-pitch pads).
- Supply rails on the outer layers only: 12 to 22 connections stay open regardless of pass count.
- Rip-up by radius around a blocked pin: open connections grew from 22 to 100 in four passes.
- Rip-up of only the crossed tracks: also grew (13 → 19 → 27). Part of that was the search limit, not yet re-tested.
