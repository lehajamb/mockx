Price-time priority in integer ticks

Every authoritative calculation in MatchingEngine uses exact integers (ticks for price, lots for quantity). From submit through match, fill, and report, no floating-point operation touches an order's quantity or price.

2.2

submit → process: the two-phase handoff

submit_order() creates the order and schedules deferred processing through the scheduler. process_order() is called later by the scheduler and contains all matching logic. Between the two, a cancel can still take effect.

submit_order(NewOrderCommand) Validates the command, creates an Order with status=PENDING_NEW, version=0, processed=False. Assigns order.id (internal) and exchange_order_id (public G{gen}-O{counter}). Registers in self.orders and self.client_order_index. engine.py:120-187
Schedule two events via scheduler.schedule_order_processing() order_arrived at T+inbound (observability only, never touches the book). order_process at T+inbound+engine (calls engine.process_order()). Each carries a ProcessOrderCommand(order_id, expected_version). engine.py:170-179, latency.py:311-345
Cancel window — Between submission and processing, a cancel_order() arriving before the scheduled order_process event can successfully cancel the order. The cancel increments order.version to 1, so when the stale order_process (expected_version=0) fires, it detects the version mismatch and drops the processing attempt. engine.py:189-203, 799

Otherwise: _run_matching() → _finalize_unfilled_remainder() → book.assert_invariants() → _send_market_data_update(). engine.py:189-228

2.4

Remainder finalization rules

After matching exhausts the book or the aggressor's price limit, any leftover quantity is resolved by _finalize_unfilled_remainder().

Market or IOC

Remainder is canceled outright

Market orders never rest, and IOC means immediate-or-cancel. Whatever quantity didn't fill immediately is canceled and reported as CANCELED — no trace left in the book.

engine.py:464-483

GTC, nothing filled

Rests, with one NEW report

A wholly unfilled GTC limit order joins its price level with book.add_order() and gets a single ExecutionType.NEW report acknowledging it's live.

engine.py:485-507

GTC, partially filled

Rests silently

The TRADE/PARTIAL reports already sent during matching are the acknowledgment — no extra NEW report follows the fills. The remainder simply rests.

2.5

Self-trade prevention (STP)

Every order carries an immutable participant_id. When an aggressor encounters a passive order with the same participant, MockX cancels both complete remainders, records no trade, emits two cancellation reports (passive first, then aggressor), and stops matching that aggressor.

Cancellation semantics

Passive order's complete remainder is canceled. Aggressor's complete remainder is canceled. Both emit CANCELED Execution Reports with text: SELF_TRADE_PREVENTION: canceled passive and aggressor; prevented_qty=.

Earlier third-party fills preserved

If the aggressor had already filled against other participants before encountering its own, those fills remain in cumulative quantity and average price. STP only cancels what remains.

STP check: engine.py:274-276 (_is_self_cross). STP execution: engine.py:278-329 (_prevent_self_trade). Called during matching: engine.py:361-368.
2.6

Cancel resolution

cancel_order() resolves against authoritative engine state at execution time, not a snapshot taken in the FIX callback. The cancel can affect pending (not yet processed) and resting (NEW/PARTIAL) orders.

2.7

Price-level accounting

Each BookLevel tracks three aggregate quantities: total level quantity, strategy quantity, and market quantity. The category is determined at order creation by the session alias and is immutable for the order's lifetime — resting quantities never move between accounting categories after placement.

Assertions on every mutation

BookLevel._updated_aggregates() verifies that level_qty = strategy_qty + market_qty before and after every fill, add, remove, or STP decrement. assert_invariants() walks all orders and recomputes totals from scratch.

Best bid < best ask

OrderBook.assert_invariants() verifies this at the end of every process_order() call. A crossed or locked book raises OrderBookInvariantError.
