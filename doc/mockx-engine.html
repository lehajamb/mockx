Matching engine

Each symbol owns price-sorted bid/ask levels with FIFO order queues. Canonical prices and quantities are integer ticks and lots. Incremental level accounting maintains total, strategy and market quantity; an authoritative active-order index supports bounded gauge refreshes. Full audits are linear and remain enabled.

New orders

Each crossing fill executes at the passive price and emits immutable passive then aggressor reports. An unmatched GTC limit rests; IOC and market remainders never rest. Live orders are NEW/PARTIAL, terminal orders are absent, aggregate quantities equal queue leaves, lookup indexes agree, and completed books are uncrossed. Exact normalization rejects off-grid and non-finite values before engine entry.

Native replacement

FIX G changes price and total intended quantity of an accepted live GTC limit. Ownership, logical OrderID and cumulative fills are preserved. Current ClOrdID advances only on success; F/G must reference it and any original ListID. Same-price reduction or no-op preserves FIFO. Price change or quantity increase loses priority and can match naturally. Total quantity is checked against processing-time CumQty; positive totals at or below CumQty clamp to CumQty and stop the remainder without inventing a trade.

priority preserved:       ITCH X reduction (or no delta for no-op)
priority lost:            D old display ref -> passive E/C fills -> A new ref if leaves rest
logical FIX OrderID:      unchanged
failed amendment:         original order and queue untouched

Self-trade prevention

Participant identity is immutable and independent of session alias. On self-cross, the passive and aggressive remainders are cancelled and matching stops. Earlier third-party fills remain in cumulative quantities; no self-trade or fake volume is recorded. Initial-order rejection probability is limited to new orders.

Publication

Engine callbacks supply immutable reports, trades and ordered native book changes to the command-effects envelope. Effects publish only after invariant checks. ITCH and persistence share those changes; there is no hot full-book serialization. REST/GLIMPSE/checkpoints capture immutable state on demand.
