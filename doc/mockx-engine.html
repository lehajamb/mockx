Price-time priority in integer ticks

Every authoritative calculation in MatchingEngine uses exact integers (ticks for price, lots for quantity). From submit through match, fill, and report, no floating-point operation touches an order's quantity or price.

PENDING_NEW → CANCELLED
Trigger: pending cancel, STP, or unfilled IOC/market remainder

Trigger: cancel_order(), STP, or IOC/market remainder
A resting or partially filled order is cancelled by request or self-trade prevention. A PARTIAL IOC/market order also cancels its unexecuted remainder.

2.2

submit → process: the two-phase handoff

submit_order() creates the order and schedules deferred processing through the scheduler. process_order() is called later by the scheduler and contains all matching logic. Between the two, a cancel can still take effect.

submit_order(NewOrderCommand) Validates the command, creates an Order with status=PENDING_NEW, version=0, processed=False. Assigns order.id (internal) and exchange_order_id (public G{gen}-O{counter}). Registers in self.orders and self.client_order_index. MatchingEngine.submit_order()
Schedule two events via scheduler.schedule_order_processing() order_arrived at T+inbound (observability only, never touches the book). order_process at T+inbound+engine (calls engine.process_order()). Each carries a ProcessOrderCommand(order_id, expected_version). LatencyScheduler.schedule_order_processing()
Cancel window — Between submission and processing, a cancel_order() arriving before the scheduled order_process event can successfully cancel the order. The cancel increments order.version to 1, so when the stale order_process (expected_version=0) fires, it detects the version mismatch and drops the processing attempt. MatchingEngine.process_order()
process_order(ProcessOrderCommand) Guards: order exists, version matches command, status is PENDING_NEW, not yet processed. Then: RNG reject check → if rejected, calls _reject_order(). Otherwise: _run_matching() → _finalize_unfilled_remainder() → book.assert_invariants() → _send_market_data_update(). MatchingEngine.process_order()
Order creation and processing: MatchingEngine.submit_order() and process_order(). Version protection is enforced by ProcessOrderCommand.expected_version and successful cancel.

2.4

Remainder finalization rules

After matching exhausts the book or the aggressor's price limit, any leftover quantity is resolved by _finalize_unfilled_remainder().

Market or IOC

Remainder is canceled outright

Market orders never rest, and IOC means immediate-or-cancel. Whatever quantity didn't fill immediately is canceled and reported as CANCELED — no trace left in the book.

MatchingEngine._finalize_unfilled_remainder()

GTC, nothing filled

Rests, with one NEW report

A wholly unfilled GTC limit order joins its price level with book.add_order() and gets a single ExecutionType.NEW report acknowledging it's live.

MatchingEngine._finalize_unfilled_remainder()

GTC, partially filled

Rests silently

The TRADE/PARTIAL reports already sent during matching are the acknowledgment — no extra NEW report follows the fills. The remainder simply rests.

2.4

Authoritative displayed-order events

ITCH is not reconstructed from client reports or trades. The engine buffers protocol-neutral events while mutating the book and publishes them in mutation order only after the command's invariant check passes.

DisplayedOrderAdded

A remainder actually rests

Published after book.add_order() for a new or partially filled GTC limit remainder. Market and IOC orders never produce it.

DisplayedOrderExecuted

One event per fill

References only the displayed passive order. Its match number is exactly Fill.trade_id; the aggressor report and Trade callback do not create feed events.

DisplayedOrderDeleted

A remainder leaves without execution

Published for a resting cancel, the displayed passive removed by STP, and a priority-losing replacement's old reference. Pending cancellation and a never-rested aggressor produce no delete.

DisplayedOrderReduced

A replacement preserves FIFO

Maps to ITCH X with the removed shares, retaining the displayed reference and FIFO position. A no-op emits no displayed event. Repricing or increasing quantity emits D, any passive E/C, then A with a fresh reference if leaves rest.

Event models: models.py. Mutation and publication: MatchingEngine._queue_displayed_event() and _publish_displayed_events(). Wire mapping: itch_delivery.py.
2.5

Self-trade prevention (STP)

Every order carries an immutable participant_id. When an aggressor encounters a passive order with the same participant, MockX cancels both complete remainders, records no trade, emits two cancellation reports (passive first, then aggressor), and stops matching that aggressor.

Cancellation semantics

Passive order's complete remainder is canceled. Aggressor's complete remainder is canceled. Both emit CANCELED Execution Reports with text: SELF_TRADE_PREVENTION: canceled passive and aggressor; prevented_qty=.

Earlier third-party fills preserved

If the aggressor had already filled against other participants before encountering its own, those fills remain in cumulative quantity and average price. STP only cancels what remains.

STP check and execution: MatchingEngine._is_self_cross() and _prevent_self_trade(), called from the matching loop.

Native replace resolution

replace_order() resolves the latest accepted client revision at engine execution time. Only live resting NEW/PARTIAL GTC limit orders qualify; the old order remains executable during request latency.

AmendmentCommit and priority
Same-price total-quantity reductionAtomically resize level aggregates and leaves; retain FIFO and displayed reference; publish ITCH X.
No change to price or total quantityAdvance accepted ClOrdID and acknowledge; preserve FIFO; publish no book snapshot or displayed event.
Quantity increase or price changeRemove old displayed order, lose FIFO, match using the existing loop and STP, and append a remainder with a fresh display reference.
Requested total at or below current CumQtyClamp total to actual CumQty, remove leaves, and finish FILLED. Preserve fills/notional; create no trade and add no canceled quantity.

OrderQty means total quantity including previous fills. Internal/public OrderID, original submission time, ownership, cumulative fills, and notional remain stable. Symbol, side, order type, TIF, FIX session, and participant cannot change. Exact tick/lot and enabled ITCH bounds apply; replacement does not draw a new random rejection.

Every accepted replacement, including a no-op, advances ClOrdID. Later F/G requests must reference that latest accepted ID; historical accepted IDs remain diagnostic entries. Rejected amendments never become active revisions, but normalized D/F/G requests share one process-lifetime session-scoped duplicate namespace.

Publish one immutable ExecutionReport with ExecType=5 and OrdStatus NEW/PARTIAL/FILLED before replacement-triggered fill reports. It carries new ClOrdID and previous accepted OrigClOrdID; later reports use the new ID. There is no persistent REPLACED state or interim Pending Replace report. Rejections use immutable OrderCancelReject with CxlRejResponseTo=2 and execution-time status; F rejects retain CxlRejResponseTo=1.

Unknown/stale references, wrong ownership, duplicates, unsupported fields, ineligible pending/terminal state, and unavailable exchange have stable reject reason/text. Validation precedes removal of liquidity. A changed book produces one final immutable snapshot; a rejection or no-op produces none. Final invariants and fail-stop handling remain mandatory.

Models: ReplaceOrderCommand, OrderCancelReject, and ReplacementOutcome. Engine: MatchingEngine.replace_order(). Accounting: OrderBook.resize_order(). FIX examples and exact counters: README “Native FIX cancel/replace” and “Metrics and observability”.
2.7

Price-level accounting

Each BookLevel tracks three aggregate quantities: total level quantity, strategy quantity, and market quantity. The category is determined at order creation by the session alias and is immutable for the order's lifetime — resting quantities never move between accounting categories after placement.

Assertions on every mutation

BookLevel._updated_aggregates() verifies that level_qty = strategy_qty + market_qty before and after every fill, add, remove, or STP decrement. assert_invariants() walks all orders and recomputes totals from scratch.

Best bid < best ask

OrderBook.assert_invariants() verifies this at the end of every process_order() call. A crossed or locked book raises OrderBookInvariantError.
